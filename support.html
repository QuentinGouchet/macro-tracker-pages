Macro Tracker — Support

Macro Tracker is a food, macro, weight, water, workout, and mood tracker that works fully offline — all of your data stays on your device.

Need help or found a bug?

Email oaklabshq@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Everything is stored locally on your device. Use the Backup option in Settings to export a copy, or turn on auto-backup to a folder you choose.

Do I need an account?
No — the app works fully offline with no sign-up required.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete all data. This is permanent and can't be undone.
