Privacy Policy — Macro Tracker

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Macro Tracker ("the app") is developed by Oak Labs. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data stored on your device

All food logs, goals, weight entries, water intake, workouts, mood entries, custom foods, favorites, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. None of this data is transmitted to us, sold, or shared with anyone. If you delete the app or use the "Delete all data" option in Settings, it's gone — we never had a copy.

If you use the optional backup feature, the backup file is created and stored either on your device or in a folder you explicitly choose (such as a cloud-synced folder you control, e.g. Google Drive). We do not have access to that file or any account associated with it.

Camera

The app requests camera access only to scan a food's barcode. Camera images are processed on your device to read the barcode and are never saved, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Food database lookups

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode or search text to public, third-party nutrition databases (such as Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central) to retrieve nutrition information. These requests do not include your name, email, or any other information that identifies you.

Purchases

If you choose to purchase the premium upgrade, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) and processed via RevenueCat for entitlement tracking. We never see or store your payment information. RevenueCat may receive a device/purchase identifier to confirm your entitlement — see RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account to use the app.
  • We don't run analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
